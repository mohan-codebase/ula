# Ula: Pearl + Botanical Ink

A quiet premium direction for Ula. Tamil Nadu's photography supplies the color and sense of place; the interface supplies space, contrast and restraint.

| Role | Color | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main background | Pearl | #FAFAF7 |
| Identity, headings, dark sections | Botanical ink | #17211D |
| Hero and footer actions, small details | Champagne | #C7B69A |
| Panels | Platinum | #ECEDE8 |
| Secondary text on light | Stone | #656962 |
| Secondary text on dark | Silver | #CFD3CD |

Most surfaces stay neutral. Use champagne sparingly, with dark text. Primary controls on light backgrounds use ink with white text. The footer is dark, with quiet silver metadata. Photo scrims are neutral, preserving the destinations' own colors. No orange actions or saturated yellow areas.

Supporting colors: ink hover #2E3D35, champagne hover #D6C9B3, dividers #D9DCD5, control border #858A82.

The earlier Coastal Ink + Temple Vermilion direction was rejected by the owner as insufficiently premium and is superseded by this palette.
